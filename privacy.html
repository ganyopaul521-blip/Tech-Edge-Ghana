Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

TechEdge Ghana ("TechEdge Ghana", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of everyone who visits this website or contacts us through it. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana, and, where our visitors are located outside Ghana, we apply comparable data protection principles (such as those under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation) as good practice.

By using this website (the "Site") or submitting information to us through it, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Site or submit your information to us.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

TechEdge Ghana is a software development firm based in Accra, Ghana. For the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), TechEdge Ghana is the data controller responsible for the personal data collected through this Site.

Data controller contact: techedgeghana@gmail.com · Accra, Ghana

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data only when you choose to give it to us — primarily through our contact / project inquiry form. Depending on what you submit, this may include:

  • Identity data: your full name.
  • Contact data: your email address and, if provided, your phone number.
  • Enquiry data: the service you are interested in and the details of your project or message.
  • Communications data: any information you share with us afterwards by email or WhatsApp while we discuss your project.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this Site, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (such as health, financial, or biometric information) through the contact form. Please avoid including sensitive personal data in your message unless it is strictly necessary for us to understand your project.

3. How We Collect Your Data

We collect personal data directly from you when you:

  • Fill in and submit the contact form on this Site;
  • Email us directly at techedgeghana@gmail.com;
  • Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram using the links provided on this Site.

Our contact form is processed using Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which securely transmits your submission to our email inbox. We do not store your form submissions in our own database; Web3Forms processes the submission solely to deliver it to us and is bound by its own privacy and security practices.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We only process your personal data where we have a valid legal basis to do so, namely:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit the contact form or message us, you consent to us using that information to respond to you.
  • Legitimate interest — to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, and maintain business communications, in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Contract performance — where you engage us for a project, to communicate about and deliver that project (governed additionally by a separate signed agreement or statement of work).
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with Ghanaian law.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use the personal data you provide only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project requirements;
  • To prepare quotes, proposals, and contracts;
  • To deliver services you have engaged us for and provide related support;
  • To maintain records of our business communications; and
  • To comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for unsolicited marketing.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate this Site. These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address or browser type) as part of delivering their service:

  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact form submissions to our email.
  • Google Fonts — loads web fonts used on this Site.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) — delivers icon assets used on this Site.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves this Site.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — if you choose to contact us via the WhatsApp chat link.
  • Instagram / Meta — if you choose to visit or message us via our Instagram page.

These providers act as independent data processors or controllers for the technical data their services require and maintain their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

7. Cookies

This Site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Some of the third-party services listed above (for example, embedded fonts or icon libraries) may load resources from their own servers, which can involve standard technical logging on their end, but TechEdge Ghana does not itself place tracking cookies on your device.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of the third-party providers listed in Section 6 operate servers outside Ghana. Where your personal data is transferred outside Ghana, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled securely and in a manner consistent with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), including relying on providers that maintain appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data collected through the contact form only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, pursue a resulting business relationship, and meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Where you engage us for a project, project-related data is retained for the duration of the engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards to support ongoing maintenance, warranty, or legal requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

10. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure, including restricting access to enquiry data to personnel who need it to respond to you. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is collected and used;
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request deletion of your data where it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
  • Object to processing, including for direct marketing purposes; and
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at techedgeghana@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

12. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for businesses and individuals seeking software development services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
