Refund, Cancellation & Payment Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

This Refund, Cancellation and Payment Policy explains the terms that apply when clients make payments for software development services, projects, or other offerings through TechEdge Ghana ("we," "us," or "our").

By making a payment to us — whether through this website, bank transfer, mobile money, or a third-party payment link we provide — you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms outlined below. This policy applies alongside our Terms of Service and any signed proposal, statement of work, or service agreement covering your specific project, which will take precedence over this general policy where they conflict.

1. Payment Terms

Payments to TechEdge Ghana must be made using the payment methods we make available for a given engagement, which may include bank transfer, mobile money, or a third-party payment provider (such as Paystack).

Where applicable, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. We do not directly store clients' complete payment-card information unless expressly stated otherwise.

You are responsible for providing accurate payment and billing information. We reserve the right to refuse, cancel, or place a payment on hold where we reasonably suspect fraud, unauthorized activity, incorrect information, or a violation of these terms.

A payment is considered successful only when confirmation has been received from our payment system or the relevant payment provider.

2. Pricing

All prices and quotes we provide are stated in the currency indicated in the relevant proposal or invoice (typically Ghana Cedis, GHS, unless otherwise agreed).

We reserve the right to change our pricing or fees for new engagements at any time. However, a price change will not affect a project or milestone that has already been confirmed and paid for, unless otherwise required by law or agreed with the client.

Where applicable, taxes, transaction charges, or other additional costs will be communicated before payment is completed.

3. Cancellation by the Client

Clients may request cancellation of a project or engagement by contacting us through the official contact details provided on this website.

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible.

Whether a cancellation qualifies for a full refund, partial refund, or no refund depends on the stage of the project, the amount of work already completed, and the terms of the applicable service agreement or statement of work.

Where work has already commenced, we will deduct reasonable costs for work already completed (including design, development, and project management time) before issuing any applicable refund of the remaining balance.

4. Cancellation by Us

We reserve the right to cancel or decline a project or engagement where necessary, including circumstances involving:

  • Suspected fraudulent or unauthorized transactions;
  • Incorrect or incomplete client information;
  • Unavailability of the requested service or required resources;
  • Technical or operational issues;
  • Failure to comply with our Terms of Service or the applicable service agreement; or
  • Circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

If we cancel a paid engagement and the client is entitled to a refund, the applicable amount will be refunded using the original payment method where reasonably possible.

5. Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the nature and stage of the project or service purchased, and will generally be set out in the relevant proposal or service agreement.

Where a refund is approved, the amount refunded will normally correspond to the value of work not yet delivered, less any reasonable costs already incurred.

Certain payments are non-refundable, including deposits or payments for services that have already been fully delivered, completed development work, customized or bespoke work, and third-party costs already incurred on the client's behalf (such as domain names, hosting, or licenses). Where applicable, any non-refundable fees will be clearly communicated before the client completes payment.

To request a refund, clients should contact us using:

A refund request may require the client to provide relevant information such as the invoice or order number, transaction reference, date of payment, and reason for the request.

We may request additional information where reasonably necessary to verify the transaction and process the request.

7. Refund Processing

Once a refund has been approved, we will initiate it within a reasonable period, typically within 7–14 business days.

The time it takes for the refunded amount to appear in the client's account may depend on the client's bank, mobile-money provider, card issuer, or other payment service provider.

We are not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions or payment processors after the refund has been successfully initiated by us.

8. Failed or Reversed Payments

If a payment fails, is reversed, or is otherwise unsuccessful, the client should not assume that a project or milestone has been successfully paid for until payment confirmation has been received.

If money has been deducted from a client's account despite a failed transaction, the client should contact us with the relevant transaction reference so that we can investigate the matter.

Where the payment provider confirms that funds were received by us, we will take reasonable steps to resolve the transaction.

9. Duplicate Payments

If you believe that you have accidentally made the same payment more than once, please contact us as soon as possible with the relevant transaction details.

After verification, any confirmed duplicate payment may be refunded in accordance with this policy.

10. Third-Party Payment Providers

Payments made to us may be processed by third-party payment providers (such as Paystack, mobile money operators, or banks).

These providers may have their own terms, conditions, privacy policies, transaction limits, processing times, and fees.

We are not responsible for delays, failures, interruptions, or restrictions caused solely by a third-party payment provider, although we will make reasonable efforts to assist clients in resolving payment-related issues.

11. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a payment was made without your authorization, you should notify us and, where appropriate, your bank, card issuer, or mobile-money/payment provider immediately.

We may investigate suspected unauthorized transactions and may request information reasonably necessary to verify the transaction.

We will not knowingly retain funds obtained through fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

12. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or payment provider, we encourage you to contact us so that we can attempt to resolve the issue directly.

Nothing in this policy prevents a client from exercising any rights available to them under applicable Ghanaian law.

13. Exceptions

Certain projects, custom or bespoke development work, promotional offers, or special engagements may be subject to different cancellation or refund terms.

Where different terms apply, those terms will be communicated to the client before or at the time of payment (typically in the relevant proposal or service agreement) and will take precedence over this general policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund, Cancellation and Payment Policy from time to time.

Any updated version will be published on this website with a revised effective date. Clients should review the policy periodically to remain informed about our current payment and refund practices.
